% -------------------- SUMMARY --------------------
\sectionBlock{
\section{Summary}}
{
Ph.D. (CIFRE) candidate in Computer Science at Sorbonne University \& EURECOM (expected graduation in Aug 2026), collaborating with Huawei Paris Research Center. My research lies at the intersection of AI (Artificial Intelligence), HPC (High Performance Computing) and MLSys (Machine Learning System), with a focus on principled, optimization-based planning for efficient and reliable ultra-scale LLM/DNN training. I develop symbolic performance/memory models and ILP/constraint-based search to synthesize memory-feasible, communication-aware hybrid-parallel strategies and pipeline schedules, with publications at CLUSTER, Euro-Par (Best Poster Award), NPC, and HPC Asia. My work is validated on production Ascend clusters up to 16,384 NPUs and informs deployable planning workflows, bridging foundational systems research and real-world training infrastructure.
\vspace{-15pt}
}


% -------------------- CORE COMPETENCIES --------------------
\sectionBlock{
\section{Research Expertise}}
{
\item Research on scalable LLM/DNN training systems: hybrid-parallel strategy design (tensor/data/pipeline/expert), stage partitioning, micro-batch scheduling.
\item Principled modeling for system co-optimization: symbolic performance and peak-memory modeling, feasibility analysis, and validation methodology for large-scale clusters.
\item Optimization-based planning algorithms: constraint programming for joint optimization of memory/communication/throughput.
\item Translation of systems research to practice: reproducible benchmarking and deployable workflows in collaboration with engineering and platform teams.
