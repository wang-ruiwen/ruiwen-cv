  \ResumeBullet{\textbf{H2O} (Euro-Par 2025, Best Poster) -- \textbf{holistic all-dimension optimisation}: co-optimises every parallelism dimension with micro-batch and adaptive recomputation in one workflow, transferred to production; MFU 26.13\% to 35.73\%.}
  \ResumeBullet{\textbf{Concord} (under submission 2026) -- \textbf{search efficiency}: dependency-keyed per-layer signatures re-plan only what a model or config change invalidates, reusing the rest and cutting redundant search.}
  \ResumeBullet{\textbf{ManuMatic} (NPC 2025) -- \textbf{adaptability to new operators}: injects pinned operator shardings as first-class constraints so plans stay robust to unmodeled operators and runtime effects, recovering strong plans where pure auto-search degrades.}
